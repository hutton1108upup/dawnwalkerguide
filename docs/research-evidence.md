## Findings that affect the MVP

- The game is released. Bandai Namco's dated September 3 announcement confirms availability on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Do not write a pre-release site. [Official launch announcement](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-is-now-available)
- Hotfix 1.0.2 is real, dated September 4. Its existence is independently verified; any unrelated patch text in a design mockup remains a mockup. [Official hotfix](https://dawnwalkergame.com/us/en/news/hotfix-102)
- Enough documented playthrough evidence exists for a small prologue quest dataset and several spoiler-controlled decisions. It does **not** establish a complete campaign database, guaranteed completionist route, or tested planner for all 30 days.
- Sources from PC Gamer below are original authored playthrough reports. Label them `documented-playthrough` / `Reported by PC Gamer`, never `official` or `tested by us`. No game build number is documented for those reports; do not silently label them patch 1.0.2 tested.

[Bandai Namco gameplay reveal recap](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dawnwalker/news/the-blood-of-dawnwalker-gameplay-reveal-recap): an hourglass previews an activity's time cost. ### P3 — Blasphemy decision

[Sean Martin, PC Gamer, September 2, 2026](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blood-of-dawnwalker-blasphemy-walkthrough/): recover Gremla's banner before the evening Blood Mass. Convincing Premysl to return it preserves Gremla's life and yields money/XP. Keeping his secret awards the Daylight Amulet but Gremla dies at Mass. The author's statement that no larger impact was seen is a limited observation, not proof that every later branch is unaffected.

Suggested spoiler layers: hidden = “A prologue decision with a reward-versus-character tradeoff”; light = “Return the banner if protecting the villager is your priority”; full = reveal the specific reward and death. Do not put the full consequence in search snippets or hidden card labels.

### P4 — Esme opening decision

[Sean Martin, PC Gamer, September 2, 2026](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blood-of-dawnwalker-should-you-force-feed-coens-mum-esme/): forcing Esme to eat and declining both continue to Pieter's request for herbs. The reported difference is the immediate emotional scene; the author reports no long-term effect. Do not mark the decision as a permanent quest lockout. Distinguish this opening choice from preparing her medicine later.

### P5 — The Heart Wants What It Wants decision

[Sean Martin, PC Gamer, September 2, 2026](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blood-of-dawnwalker-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-walkthrough/): Ocha and Nora give the quest at Altenmarkt inn. The ring is the correct memento for Ocha's spell. Directly fighting Andrei or giving the wrong memento causes his death in combat; the successful spell leads to a different death and preserves an option for Ocha to stay in the city. After the spell path, encouraging new experiences supports Ocha taking over the city operation with Nora, teaching Swordmastery and opening Study in Crimson. Advice favoring home sends her to the mountains. With a combat death, the non-home alternative is travelling with Nora. Broad Swing is the reported quest reward. Total time cost is **unknown** in this source. Do not claim the ring spares Andrei; it does not. The writer explicitly had not confirmed whether Ocha teaches at the mountain settlement.

### P6 — playthrough planning context

[Sean Martin's 80-hour tips, September 2, 2026](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blood-of-dawnwalker-tips/): exploration and ordinary enemy hunting do not spend the narrative clock. Sun/moon icons identify activities available only in the matching phase. Some white dialogue leads to additional consequential options. New opportunities can appear in previously explored areas. His remaining 15 days at story completion is one player's result, not a guaranteed time budget or 100% route.

### P7 — romance scope

[Lauren Morton, PC Gamer romance report](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blood-of-dawnwalker-romances/): reports Anca, Crake and Lacra as romanceable; more than one partner is possible. Follow each character's questline and pay attention to optional dialogue. The article does not say every listed flirt is mandatory. Anca has a longer storyline; calling her “canon” would promote editorial interpretation to game fact. Use this as a sourced overview and link to the full original report for detailed sequences.

### O3 — official issue handling

[Hotfix 1.0.2, September 4, 2026](https://dawnwalkergame.com/us/en/news/hotfix-102): deployed on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Notes address Farkas dialogue, Lunka tutorial navigation, Uriashi blacksmith crafting and PC controller/sprint input. Known issues still listed include windowed/borderless stutter (try Full Screen), shader-compilation crashes (BIOS guidance linked by the developer), and missing PS5 gamepad mapping. Do not state that 1.0.2 fixed the known-issues section. Do not promise an FPS gain or general crash cure. For firmware guidance, link to the official notice and hardware manufacturer's instructions; do not prescribe undocumented BIOS or voltage changes.

### O4 — PC requirements

[Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3751260/The_Blood_of_Dawnwalker/): minimum i5-11400F / Ryzen 7 2700X, GTX 1060 / RX 580, 16 GB RAM, 60 GB SSD, DX12, Windows 10; recommended i7-11700K / Ryzen 7 5700X, RTX 4060 / RX 7600 XT / Arc B580, 16 GB RAM, 60 GB SSD. Store lists 6 GB minimum / 8 GB recommended VRAM. These are requirements, not measured frame rates. Steam's displayed September 2 date differs from the publisher's September 3 launch announcement; retain the publisher date and avoid asserting a universal timezone conversion without a separate unlock source.

## Deployment issue discovered during source checks

[Official community guidelines](https://dawnwalkergame.com/us/en/community) currently ask fan/community sites to identify themselves as unofficial and avoid game/company names or similar trademarks in their domains. They also restrict commercial uses of game content, with specific video/stream exceptions. The requested `dawnwalker-guide.wiki` conflicts with the published domain wording. This is a concrete official-guideline issue, not a determination of legal infringement. Continue the authorized local build; report this before public deployment and avoid implying a disclaimer alone resolves the domain requirement. Do not automatically change the user's domain or contact anyone.

Recheck detail: this appears under Content Guidelines Library, Golden rules, item 3, and explicitly addresses community-run websites/fanpages rather than only contest entrants. Item 1 addresses game content and content based on the game; it does not expressly grant ad-supported guide sites a commercial exception. The page says other legal documents/EULA take precedence. Its introduction still says the game is unreleased, so the document is visibly not fully refreshed; its actual revision/effective date is not shown. That limits conclusions about legal enforceability but does not remove the live published domain condition. No legal advice or rights-holder approval has been obtained.

## Editorial implementation guardrails

- Store source URL, source type, checked date, platform/build if actually known, and confidence separately.
- An unknown numeric cost is null, never zero. Conditional cost needs a visible reason and range.
- A subtotal of selected activities is not a simulation of every prerequisite, phase switch or lockout.
- Recommendations may be our own goal-based editorial judgments; label them as such and expose the factual basis.
- Keep extracted facts concise. Each external article should supply a short summary, not a rewritten replacement for its entire walkthrough; no copied screenshots or extended dialogue scripts are required for this MVP.
